07 — Voice & usage

The rules

Blue is the only money colour

Reserve --main for the primary action, active states and links. One blue CTA per view.

Yellow = Pro

Pro tints, locked presets and premium badges are always yellow — never use it for general emphasis.

Borders on everything

Every surface, control and chip gets a 2px black border. Shadows are hard offsets — never blurred.

Privacy is the pitch

"Processed entirely in your browser, never uploaded" stays the recurring line. Plain, confident copy — speak to "you".
