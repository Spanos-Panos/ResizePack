Your images stay on your device

ResizePack processes your images entirely in your browser. Your images are not uploaded to our servers, and processed files are downloaded directly to your device as a ZIP. We never see, store, or transmit your image content.

Accounts

Creating an account is optional and only needed for the Pro plan. If you sign up, our authentication provider (Supabase) stores your email address and an account identifier. If you use Google or GitHub sign-in, we receive your email and basic profile from that provider — not your password.

Payments

Pro subscriptions are handled by Stripe. Stripe processes your payment and card details — we never receive or store your full card information. We keep only your subscription status, billing period, and a Stripe customer/subscription identifier so we can show your plan and let you manage billing.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled, they are limited to basic, privacy-conscious traffic metrics (for example page views and referrers) via Google Analytics. No image data is ever collected. If analytics are not configured, no analytics scripts load.

Service providers (data processors)

  • Vercel — hosts the website.
  • Render — hosts the billing API.
  • Supabase — authentication and account/subscription database.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Google — optional analytics and (if used) Google sign-in.
