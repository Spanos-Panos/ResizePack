Subscription billing

ResizePack Pro is billed monthly in advance through Stripe. Your subscription renews automatically each period until you cancel.

Cancelling

You can cancel any time from Account → Manage billing (the Stripe customer portal). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep Pro access until then, and you are not charged again.

Refunds

Because cancellation lets you keep access for the period you already paid for, we generally do not provide partial refunds for unused time.

If you were charged in error, experienced a genuine problem, or have a statutory right to a refund (for example under EU consumer rules), contact us and we will review your case promptly and in good faith.
