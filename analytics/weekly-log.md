# ResizePack — Weekly Metrics Log

Update every Monday for the **prior** week. The point is to watch *trends over
weeks*, not react to daily noise. Always exclude your own test accounts from
"real signups".

## What to capture each week
- **Search Console** (Performance): impressions, clicks, avg position.
- **Analytics** (once added): visitors, top pages, referrers, `batch_processed`
  events (did people actually use the tool?), free vs Pro.
- **Signups:** new *real* accounts only (exclude your own).
- **Revenue:** active subscriptions / one-off (Stripe).
- **Shipped:** what changed in the product.
- **Marketing:** what you posted / where.
- **Learning + next focus.**
